## Choreography

The 20-second clock staggers four journeys by five seconds. A shared fork lowers each source onto the moving transport surface. Sources continue toward the scanner while prior findings expand, travel upward and enter the lifting receiver. The receiver descends with the context and retains a source-linked folio. At least one source and one result remain in motion through every phase; at most one result occupies the main reading size. Source and receiver carriers share the same timeline as their payloads.

The animation is silent and has no controls. It pauses offscreen and in hidden documents; reduced motion shows a complete still composition. The understanding explorer below remains independent. One muted video decoder shares the site's media playback controller. The clock mutates transforms and opacity without React renders on every frame. Source text and chart values remain in findings; no process labels are added.
