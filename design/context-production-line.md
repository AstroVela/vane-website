# Homepage production line

The composition follows the user-approved Hypit reference: a narrow conveyor, a
vertical product gate, two linked mechanical grips and three open boxes. Original
video, image, audio and PDF cards retain the established neutral frames and assets.
Cards are clipped at the gate: their original content disappears into its left side;
the matching cited understanding emerges on its right. The right grip picks up the
finding and releases it into one of the three agent boxes. The original and result
video share one decoded stream. The animation is silent and has no controls.

All geometry uses one 2900 × 1631 coordinate system and one clock, including belt
stripes, cards, grips and delivery. Transport matches the reference's approximate
144/175 native pixels per second. Linked joint geometry places the fingers at the
same coordinates used for the card. Motion pauses offscreen and in hidden tabs;
reduced motion retains a static scene. Existing manual media playback takes priority.

## Reference assets

Source: https://hypit.ai/ (conveyor/sprites, version 22dcbbb2), used at the user's
explicit request to reproduce the reference apparatus. Local assets are under
public/showcase/context/production/. The plate, pillar, arm components and belt masks
are displayed with neutral filters. The original product/platform markings are
covered by the Vane gate face and three new agent faces. Screen content comes only
from the site's existing media. The animation module is implemented locally and does
not import the reference website's application code.
